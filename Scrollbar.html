Essay: Picnic

Last winter holidays, my family decided to visit the botanical garden in our city. My parents, sister, grandparents, and I left early in the morning carrying a basket filled with freshly prepared food, juice, and snacks. As we entered the garden, we were enchanted by the beautiful flowers, green grass, and chirping birds. Grandfather showed us rare plants, while grandmother pointed out different types of butterflies. My sister and I loved running on the soft grass and playing with a ball. Later, we played musical chairs with our parents. Around noon, we laid out our lunch on a colorful mat. The food tasted heavenly, and we shared jokes and laughter. After resting, dad organized a treasure hunt for us—my favorite part of the day. We clicked family photos to remember these special moments. The fresh air and lovely surroundings made us forget all worries. By sunset, we felt relaxed and happy. On our way home, we talked about our favorite activities and lessons learnt from nature. Family picnics let us bond, talk, and make lasting memories away from screens. They also remind us to respect and care for our environment. I hope to organize more picnics with my family, as they make me feel loved and joyful every single time.
